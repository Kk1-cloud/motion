# Brand / launch reel

```
Make a dynamic [DURATION]-second motion graphics video for [PRODUCT] ([URL]) for [AUDIENCE] on
[CHANNEL, sound on/off]. Energy of a motion designer's showreel. Go all out.

Assets
- Visit the site. Capture real screenshots (Playwright), the real logo, real colors and fonts.
  Save to films/<name>/assets and list what you found before you animate.
- Never redraw the product UI from imagination. Crop and animate the real thing.

Story (one beat each, 2 to 4 seconds, on a 120 BPM grid)
1. Hook: [THE PROBLEM IN 5 WORDS] as huge kinetic type. Frame 1 already has it.
2. The product appears; the UI assembles itself piece by piece.
3. [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2], [FEATURE 3]: each a UI moment with a cursor doing a real action.
   (Max 3. A 4th feature is noise.)
4. Proof: [ONE REAL NUMBER, with source].
5. Lockup + [SPECIFIC CTA, e.g. "Try it free at x.com"].

Look: [NAMED REFERENCE or ./refs/frame.png], take [palette/type/pacing], not [subject].
One accent: [HEX]. Banned: centered title on gradient, everything fading in, corner labels, glows.

Sound: original score at 120 BPM synthesized in code (or ./audio/track.wav, measured).
UI clicks and whooshes on beats.

Format: 1080x1920 first, then 1:1 and 16:9 from the same timeline (reframed, not cropped).
Gate: show me the shot list and a contact sheet of one still per beat before the full render.
```
Keep one session per brand: the second video reuses the renderer, assets and sound, and is faster.
